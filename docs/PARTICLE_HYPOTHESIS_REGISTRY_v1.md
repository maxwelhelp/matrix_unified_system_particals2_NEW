# Particle Hypothesis Registry v1

This file is the working registry of particle / jet-substructure hypotheses generated by the causal atlas.

The goal is to keep research disciplined: every hypothesis must have evidence, controls, risks, and next validation steps.

## Evidence levels

- `OBSERVED`: visible in one validated run, not yet stable.
- `CAUSAL_PATCHED`: supported by a causal patch or ablation.
- `STABLE`: reproduced across larger samples / heldout files.
- `PHYSICS_CANDIDATE`: stable and compared against known observables.
- `DISCOVERY_READY`: not allowed yet; requires external validation and expert review.

Current validated model: `ParticleNet_kinpid.pt`.

Current invalid / paused model for claims: `ParT_*`, because current direct prediction is around random accuracy.

## H1 — EdgeConv learned-neighborhood separator

**Claim:** ParticleNet separates JetClass categories mainly through dynamic graph message passing, especially EdgeConv layer 1 and layer 0, rather than only through final classifier weights.

**Current status:** `CAUSAL_PATCHED`

**Evidence:**

- `edge_conv_zero layer 1` drops accuracy near random.
- `edge_conv_zero layer 0` also drops accuracy near random.
- EdgeConv pseudo-head groups show concentrated causal channel groups, especially `L1:ch16:32`.

**Risks:**

- Full-layer zero patches are coarse.
- EdgeConv channel slices are fixed slices, not yet learned activation clusters.

**Next tests:**

1. Learned clustering of EdgeConv activations.
2. Per-class EdgeConv pseudo-head map.
3. Heldout stability across more ROOT files.
4. Route-specific edge/neighbor controls.

## H2 — EdgeConv pseudo-head specialization

**Claim:** EdgeConv contains specialized channel groups that behave like internal heads, with different causal roles across classes.

**Current status:** `OBSERVED / CAUSAL_PATCHED`

**Evidence:**

- v5 identified strong pseudo-head groups:
  - `L1:ch16:32`
  - `L2:ch224:256`
  - `L0:ch40:48`
  - `L1:ch112:128`
  - `L0:ch24:32`

**Risks:**

- Channel slices are arbitrary equal partitions.
- Need activation clustering to avoid artificial grouping.

**Next tests:**

1. Cluster EdgeConv activations by channel covariance / class-conditioned response.
2. Patch learned clusters, not fixed slices.
3. Compare cluster effects against random channel groups.

## H3 — Wqq compact route vs Tbqq wide route

**Claim:** The model distinguishes W-like and top-like jets partly through learned particle-neighbor route width. `Wqq` has compact neighbor routing, while `Tbqq` has wider neighbor routing.

**Current status:** `OBSERVED`

**Evidence:**

- KNN/EdgeConv route stats show:
  - `Wqq` has the lowest mean neighbor ΔR.
  - `Tbqq` has the highest mean neighbor ΔR.
- This pattern appears across EdgeConv layers 0, 1, and 2.
- Known observables show `Tbqq` has higher mass and particle multiplicity than `Wqq`.

**Risks:**

- Descriptive route stats are not causal route patches yet.
- Route width may proxy jet mass / multiplicity rather than a new mechanism.

**Next tests:**

1. Route-group causal patch: compact vs wide neighbor routes.
2. Heldout stability.
3. Control for `jet_sdmass` and `jet_nparticles`.
4. Compare `Wqq` vs `Zqq`, `Tbqq` vs `Tbl`.

## H4 — Leading-particle core hypothesis

**Claim:** Leading high-pT / high-energy particles are causally important for ParticleNet predictions; they are not interchangeable with random particles.

**Current status:** `CAUSAL_PATCHED`

**Evidence:**

- Removing top-pT / top-energy particles drops accuracy much more than random particle removal.

**Risks:**

- Top-pT and top-energy sets overlap heavily.
- Need compare top-k sizes and class-specific effects.

**Next tests:**

1. Top-k sweep: k=1,2,4,8,16.
2. Per-class top-particle ablation.
3. Compare with jet mass / tau variables.

## H5 — Explicit feature-channel class codes

**Claim:** Different classes depend on different explicit particle features.

**Current status:** `CAUSAL_PATCHED`

**Evidence examples from v5:**

- `Wqq` and `Zqq`: strong dependence on `part_logptrel`.
- `Tbqq`: strong dependence on `part_deltaR`.
- `Hqql`: strong dependence on `part_pt_log`.
- `Hbb/Hcc`: strong dependence on `part_logerel`.

**Risks:**

- Single-channel zeroing may create out-of-distribution inputs.
- Need compare against feature permutation and noise controls.

**Next tests:**

1. Permutation controls per channel.
2. Noise controls per channel.
3. Class-contrast reports.
4. Combine feature controls with route controls.

## H6 — Known-observable alignment

**Claim:** Some learned route patterns align with known observables like jet mass, multiplicity, and N-subjettiness.

**Current status:** `OBSERVED`

**Evidence:**

- `Tbqq` shows high `jet_sdmass` and `jet_nparticles`.
- Route width is larger for `Tbqq` than `Wqq`.

**Risks:**

- Alignment with known observables may mean the network is not using a new mechanism, only rediscovering known physics.

**Next tests:**

1. Regress route stats on known observables.
2. Residual analysis: what remains after controlling for mass/multiplicity/tau variables?
3. Compare correct vs wrong predictions.
